IPH-2.3 · Banking · Payments & Clearing

Instant Payments Hub — SCT Inst Onboarding

Sample analysis — a method demonstration, not client work. Delivery of a real-time SEPA Instant Credit Transfer (SCT Inst) capability for retail and SME customers, replacing the batch-based legacy transfer engine. The hub exposes a synchronous payment initiation API, performs real-time sanctions and fraud screening, and settles through the TIPS gateway within the 10-second scheme deadline.

1 · Business requirements24

REQ-001 Initiate SEPA Instant Credit Transfer from digital channels

The Instant Payments Hub must accept a payment initiation request from the web and mobile banking channels and execute it as an SCT Inst transaction. The request captures debtor account, creditor IBAN and name, amount in EUR, and an optional remittance reference of up to 140 characters. The hub orchestrates validation, screening, funds reservation and scheme submission synchronously and returns a final status to the channel.

Why: Retail and SME customers expect euro payments to reach the beneficiary immediately, at any hour. The legacy batch engine only executes four times per business day, driving complaints and attrition to challenger banks.

Critical · Must · Implemented · Payment Initiation · v2.3 · 16 May 2025

  • AC-001-1 Given an authenticated retail customer with an active current account, when they submit a valid instant payment of EUR 250.00 to a reachable IBAN, then the payment is settled and a final ACCP status with the scheme transaction reference is returned within 10 seconds
  • AC-001-2 Given a payment request with a remittance reference exceeding 140 characters, when the channel submits the request, then the hub rejects it with error code IPH-VAL-014 and no funds are reserved

REQ-002 Verification of Payee before payment submission

Before a payment is submitted to the scheme, the hub must call the Verification of Payee service to compare the creditor name supplied by the customer against the name registered at the beneficiary bank. The service returns MATCH, CLOSE_MATCH (with the registered name) or NO_MATCH. The channel must present the outcome to the customer and require an explicit confirmation for anything other than MATCH.

Why: The EU Instant Payments Regulation mandates a payee verification service. Misdirected and APP-fraud payments cost the bank EUR 2.4m in the previous financial year.

Critical · Must · Implemented · Validation & Screening · v2.3 · 30 Apr 2025

  • AC-002-1 Given a creditor name that differs only by a middle initial from the registered name, when Verification of Payee is invoked, then a CLOSE_MATCH is returned with the registered name and the customer is asked to confirm or amend
  • AC-002-2 Given the Verification of Payee service is unavailable, when the customer submits a payment, then the customer is warned that verification could not be performed and must accept the risk before submission

REQ-003 Real-time sanctions screening within the payment path

Every outbound and inbound instant payment must be screened against consolidated sanctions and watch lists before settlement. Screening is synchronous with a hard response budget of 1,200 ms. A confirmed hit places the payment in a compliance hold queue and the customer receives a neutral 'under review' message that does not tip off the subject.

Why: Funds Transfer Regulation 2015/847 requires screening of all payment parties before execution. Irrevocable instant settlement removes any opportunity for post-execution intervention.

Critical · Must · Implemented · Validation & Screening · v2.3 · 02 May 2025

  • AC-003-1 Given a creditor name matching an EU consolidated list entry, when the payment is screened, then the payment is held, no funds leave the account, and a compliance alert is raised within 5 seconds
  • AC-003-2 Given the screening platform does not respond within 1,200 ms, when the circuit breaker threshold is reached, then the payment is routed to the manual review queue rather than being auto-approved

REQ-004 Real-time fraud scoring and decisioning

Each outbound payment must be scored by the behavioural fraud engine using customer history, device signals, beneficiary novelty and velocity. The returned score (0–100) drives an automated decision: allow, step-up authentication, or block with referral to the fraud operations team.

Why: Instant, irrevocable payments materially increase authorised push payment fraud exposure. The bank must intervene before settlement, not after.

High · Must · Implemented · Validation & Screening · v2.3 · 18 Apr 2025

  • AC-004-1 Given a payment scored at 72 by the fraud engine, when the decision rules are evaluated, then the customer is challenged with step-up authentication before the payment continues
  • AC-004-2 Given a payment scored at 91, when the decision rules are evaluated, then the payment is blocked, a fraud case is created and the customer is directed to the fraud contact number

REQ-005 Two-phase funds reservation on the core ledger

The hub reserves the payment amount on the debtor account before submitting to TIPS. On a positive scheme confirmation the reservation is converted to a booked debit. On rejection, timeout or any downstream failure the reservation is released automatically. A compensating job releases orphaned reservations older than 60 seconds.

Why: Funds must be guaranteed before scheme submission, but must never be trapped if the scheme rejects or times out.

Critical · Must · Implemented · Settlement · v2.3 · 28 Mar 2025

  • AC-005-1 Given an accepted scheme confirmation, when the hub receives the ACCP status, then the reservation is converted to a booked debit with the scheme reference in the ledger narrative
  • AC-005-2 Given no scheme response within 25 seconds, when the reservation timer expires, then the reservation is released and the available balance is restored

REQ-006 Submit payment to TIPS within the scheme deadline

The hub must construct a pacs.008 ISO 20022 message and submit it to the TIPS gateway, correlating the pacs.002 response by end-to-end identification. The overall time budget is allocated as: validation 300 ms, VoP 1,500 ms, screening 1,200 ms, fraud 800 ms, reservation 400 ms, scheme round trip 4,000 ms.

Why: The SCT Inst rulebook requires the originating bank to complete the end-to-end flow within 10 seconds or abandon the transaction.

Critical · Must · Implemented · Settlement · v2.3 · 14 Mar 2025

  • AC-006-1 Given a validated and funded payment, when the pacs.008 is submitted, then a pacs.002 response is correlated and persisted against the payment within the 10-second budget

REQ-007 Handle scheme timeout and negative confirmation

Where TIPS returns a negative pacs.002 or no response within the deadline, the hub must set the payment to a deterministic terminal state, release the reservation, present a clear channel message with the scheme reason code translated to plain language, and offer the customer a retry or a standard SCT fallback.

Why: Ambiguous payment outcomes are the largest driver of duplicate payments and customer complaints in real-time schemes.

High · Must · Implemented · Recall & Exceptions · v2.3 · 11 Apr 2025

  • AC-007-1 Given a pacs.002 with reason code AC04 (closed account), when the response is processed, then the payment status becomes RJCT, funds are released, and the customer sees 'The beneficiary account is closed'

REQ-008 Receive and credit inbound instant payments

The hub must accept inbound pacs.008 messages from TIPS, validate the creditor account, screen the parties, credit the account with immediate value and return a pacs.002 acceptance within 5 seconds. Rejections must use the correct scheme reason codes.

Why: Scheme reachability requires the bank to accept inbound SCT Inst payments 24/7 and credit the beneficiary immediately.

Critical · Must · Implemented · Settlement · v2.3 · 25 Apr 2025

  • AC-008-1 Given an inbound payment to an active current account, when the message is validated and screened clean, then the account is credited with immediate availability and a positive pacs.002 is returned
  • AC-008-2 Given an inbound payment to a closed account, when the message is validated, then a negative pacs.002 with reason code AC04 is returned and no credit is posted

REQ-009 Beneficiary bank reachability check

Before initiating, the hub must check the beneficiary BIC against the daily TIPS reachability directory. Unreachable institutions must be identified in the channel before the customer confirms, with an automatic offer to send the payment as a standard SCT instead.

Why: Not all SEPA banks are reachable for instant payments. Attempting an unreachable payment wastes the customer's time and creates avoidable rejections.

High · Must · Implemented · Validation & Screening · v2.3 · 19 Feb 2025

  • AC-009-1 Given a beneficiary BIC absent from the reachability directory, when the customer enters the IBAN, then the channel displays that instant payment is unavailable and offers standard SCT with the next value date

REQ-010 Customer instant payment limits

Configurable per-transaction and rolling 24-hour cumulative limits apply per customer segment. Customers may view their limits and remaining allowance in the channel, and may request a temporary uplift subject to step-up authentication and a fraud review.

Why: The bank must contain fraud exposure per customer while allowing genuine high-value payments through a controlled uplift path.

High · Must · Implemented · Limits & Controls · v2.3 · 07 Apr 2025

  • AC-010-1 Given a retail customer with a EUR 15,000 daily limit who has already sent EUR 14,500, when they attempt a further payment of EUR 800, then the payment is rejected with IPH-LIM-002 and the remaining allowance of EUR 500 is displayed

REQ-011 Enforce the EUR 100,000 scheme cap

The hub must reject any instant payment request with an amount greater than EUR 100,000.00 at the validation stage, before any downstream call, and present the standard SCT alternative in the channel.

Why: Payments above the scheme maximum are rejected by TIPS and must never be submitted.

Critical · Must · Implemented · Limits & Controls · v2.3 · 30 Jan 2025

  • AC-011-1 Given a payment request of EUR 100,000.01, when the request is validated, then it is rejected with IPH-VAL-001 within 300 ms and no downstream service is called

REQ-012 Payment status enquiry

The hub must expose a status enquiry operation returning the current payment state, scheme reference, timestamps for each processing stage, and the translated reason code where applicable. Results are available to the channel, the contact centre desktop and the operations console.

Why: Contact centre agents and customers need an authoritative, real-time view of a payment's state to resolve queries at first contact.

Medium · Must · Implemented · Servicing · v2.3 · 22 Apr 2025

  • AC-012-1 Given a payment identifier for a settled payment, when the status enquiry is called, then the response contains status ACCP, the scheme reference and the settlement timestamp

REQ-013 Raise an SCT Inst recall request

Authorised users must be able to raise a recall for a settled instant payment using a permitted scheme reason code (duplicate payment, technical problem, fraudulent origin). The hub generates the camt.056 message, tracks the beneficiary bank's response and updates the case accordingly.

Why: Customers who send a payment in error, and fraud operations acting on a confirmed scam, need a scheme-compliant route to request the funds back.

High · Should · Implemented · Recall & Exceptions · v2.3 · 09 May 2025

  • AC-013-2 Given a payment settled 12 banking days ago, when a recall is attempted, then the request is rejected with IPH-RCL-003 as the scheme window has closed

REQ-014 Process inbound recall requests

Inbound camt.056 messages must create a recall case, place a hold on the disputed amount where the funds remain available, notify the beneficiary customer where legally permitted, and generate the camt.029 response with the agreed or rejected outcome.

Why: The scheme obliges the beneficiary bank to answer recall requests within 10 banking days; failure attracts scheme penalties.

High · Should · Approved · Recall & Exceptions · v2.3 · 09 May 2025

REQ-015 Customer notification for instant credits and debits

The hub must publish notification events for settled outbound payments, received inbound payments and rejected payments. The notification service delivers push, in-app and optional SMS messages using the customer's contact preferences, with quiet-hours suppression for non-urgent messages.

Why: Immediate confirmation reduces contact centre volume and gives customers assurance that irrevocable funds have moved.

4 · Functional specification8

FS-001 Payment Initiation & Request Intake

Defines how a payment instruction enters the hub from the digital channels, the canonical request contract and the synchronous orchestration sequence.

  1. The channel submits a payment instruction to POST /payments with an idempotency key that is unique per customer instruction.
  2. The hub assigns an internal payment identifier (IPH-P-<yyyymmdd>-<sequence>) and persists the request in state RCVD before any downstream call.
  3. Orchestration is strictly sequential: structural validation → reachability → Verification of Payee → limit evaluation → fraud scoring → sanctions screening → funds reservation → scheme submission.
  4. Any stage returning a blocking outcome terminates the flow immediately; stages already completed are compensated in reverse order.
  5. The final status is returned synchronously to the channel; the hub never leaves a channel request unanswered, defaulting to RJCT with reason TIMEOUT after 25 seconds.

FS-002 Verification of Payee

Beneficiary name matching against the account-holding institution prior to submission, including degraded-mode behaviour.

  1. Verification of Payee is invoked with the creditor IBAN and the customer-entered creditor name.
  2. The service returns MATCH, CLOSE_MATCH with the registered name, NO_MATCH, or NOT_SUPPORTED where the beneficiary bank does not offer the service.
  3. MATCH permits straight-through submission. CLOSE_MATCH requires the customer to confirm or amend. NO_MATCH blocks first use of a beneficiary and displays a scam warning.
  4. Results are cached for 24 hours per IBAN and name pair to avoid repeated lookups for recurring payments.
  5. Where the service is unavailable the customer is warned that verification could not be performed and must positively accept the risk; the acceptance is recorded in the audit trail.

FS-003 Screening, Fraud Scoring & Decisioning

In-path financial crime controls, their latency budgets, decision bands and the tip-off-safe customer messaging.

  1. Fraud scoring is invoked before sanctions screening so that obviously fraudulent payments never consume screening capacity.
  2. The fraud engine receives amount, beneficiary novelty, device fingerprint, session behaviour and 30-day velocity features and returns a score from 0 to 100.
  3. Score bands: 0–59 allow, 60–84 step-up authentication then re-evaluate, 85–100 block and create a fraud case.
  4. Sanctions screening covers debtor name, creditor name, creditor address and any unstructured remittance text against the consolidated list set.
  5. A screening hit sets HELD_COMPLIANCE, retains the reservation and displays a neutral 'we are completing some checks' message with no indication that screening occurred.
  6. Where screening exceeds 1,200 ms the circuit breaker routes the payment to manual review; automatic approval on timeout is explicitly prohibited.

FS-004 Funds Reservation & Ledger Posting

Two-phase commit against the core banking ledger, timer-based release and the compensating reconciliation job.

  1. The hub calls the ledger reservation API with the payment amount, the internal payment identifier and a 25-second expiry.
  2. The reservation reduces the available balance immediately but does not create an accounting entry.
  3. On a positive pacs.002 the reservation is converted to a booked debit carrying the scheme reference in the narrative.
  4. On rejection, timeout or downstream failure the reservation is released and the available balance is restored within 1 second.
  5. A compensating job runs every 60 seconds to release reservations older than 60 seconds that have no terminal payment state, and raises an operational alert for each occurrence.
  6. Inbound payments post an immediate credit with the settlement timestamp as the value date; no availability delay is permitted.

FS-005 Scheme Messaging & Status Management

ISO 20022 message construction, correlation, the payment state model and reason code translation.

  1. Outbound payments are expressed as pacs.008.001.08 and correlated to the pacs.002.001.10 response by end-to-end identification and message identification.
  2. The payment state model is RCVD → VALD → SCRN → RSVD → SUBM → ACCP | RJCT | HELD, with CANC reachable only from COOLING_OFF.
  3. Scheme reason codes are translated into customer-facing text through a maintained mapping table owned by Payments Operations.
  4. Stage timestamps are captured for every transition and exposed through the status enquiry operation and the operations dashboard.
  5. Terminal states are immutable; any subsequent scheme message creates a linked exception record rather than mutating the payment.

FS-006 Limits & Authentication Controls

Segment limit evaluation, temporary uplift handling and PSD2 Strong Customer Authentication including exemptions.

  1. Limits are evaluated after Verification of Payee and before fraud scoring so that limit rejections are cheap and fast.
  2. The rolling 24-hour window counts payments in ACCP and PDNG states; rejected and cancelled payments do not consume the allowance.
  3. A temporary uplift request requires step-up authentication, is capped at 48 hours and is subject to a fraud review for amounts above EUR 25,000.
  4. SCA applies dynamic linking: the challenge presents the exact amount and beneficiary name, and the authentication code is invalidated by any change to either value.
  5. Exemption usage is tracked per customer; the cumulative exempted amount and consecutive exemption counter reset on the next successful SCA.

FS-007 Recall & Exception Handling

Outbound and inbound recall lifecycle, permitted reason codes, scheme deadlines and operational case management.

  1. A recall is raised as camt.056 quoting the original end-to-end identification, the settlement date and a permitted reason code.
  2. Only DUPL (duplicate), TECH (technical problem) and FRAD (fraudulent origin) are accepted; any other code is rejected before submission.
  3. The recall window is 10 banking days from settlement, calculated on the TARGET2 calendar.
  4. Inbound recalls place a hold on the disputed amount where funds remain available and create an operations case with a 10 banking day response clock.
  5. The camt.029 response carries either full return, partial return with the recovered amount, or rejection with a scheme reason code.
  6. All recall decisions require four-eyes approval and are written to the immutable audit trail.

FS-008 Reconciliation, Audit & Reporting

Daily settlement reconciliation, break classification, audit record content and regulatory retention.

  1. The camt.053 settlement statement is retrieved from TIPS at 05:30 CET and matched against internal payment records by scheme reference.
  2. Breaks are classified as MISSING_AT_SCHEME, MISSING_INTERNALLY, AMOUNT_MISMATCH, LATE_CONFIRMATION or DUPLICATE_SETTLEMENT.
  3. The exception report is published to Payments Operations by 07:00 CET and every break must be assigned within the same banking day.
  4. Audit records are append-only, capture the applied rule and model versions, and are retained for 10 years from the payment date.
  5. Operations console actions, including four-eyes approvals, are recorded with the user identity, timestamp and business justification.
